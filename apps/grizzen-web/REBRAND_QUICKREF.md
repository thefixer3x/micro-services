## 💡 Brand Voice Guidelines

✅ **DO:**
- Be clear, direct, no jargon
- Focus on outcomes (save time, reduce risk)
- Use real B2B use cases
- Emphasize automation & compliance
- Show ROI/cost savings

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use African/emerging market messaging
- Emphasize personal finance/remittance
- Overcomplicate with fintech jargon
- Make promises about speed alone
- Ignore compliance/security
